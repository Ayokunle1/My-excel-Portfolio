# Adidas Sales Data Analysis — Capstone Project

## 📌 Executive Summary
Adidas operates across major retail partners (Foot Locker, Walmart, Sports Direct) through in-store, outlet, and online channels. While raw transactional data was consistently collected, a lack of consolidated analytics hindered strategic decision-making around pricing, inventory allocation, and channel performance.

This project delivers a comprehensive analysis of sales performance and profitability. By transforming raw sales data into actionable metrics and dynamic visualizations, this tool identifies key revenue drivers, channel preferences, and underperforming regional markets to guide future promotional campaigns.

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions Addressed
* **Product Performance: Which product categories (Footwear, Apparel, Accessories) generate the highest sales revenue and profit margins?
* **Retailer Contributions: How do sales compare across major retail partners like Foot Locker, Walmart, and Sports Direct?
* **Channel Analysis: Which sales method (In-Store, Outlet, Online) yields the highest revenue and volume?
* **Geographic Trends: Which regions and store locations consistently deliver top-tier sales performance versus those requiring corrective action?
* **Temporal Patterns: How do sales volume and profitability fluctuate monthly, quarterly, and seasonally?
* **Growth & Decline: Which specific product lines show strong expansion potential versus those experiencing declining demand?

---

## 🛠️ Excel Technical Features Used
* **Data Transformation & Cleaning:** `XLOOKUP`, `INDEX/MATCH`, `SUMIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS`, and Power Query for multi-source data consolidation.
* **Summarization & Analytics:** Dynamic Pivot Tables, Custom Calculated Fields, and Data Modeling (Power Pivot).
* **Interactive Dashboard:** Dynamic Pivot Charts, Timeline Slicers, Multi-field Slicers, and Conditional Formatting for KPI monitoring.
